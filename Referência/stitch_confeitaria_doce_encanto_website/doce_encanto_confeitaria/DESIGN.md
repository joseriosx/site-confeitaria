## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an evocative, warm, and sophisticated atmosphere suited for artisanal pastry, gourmet desserts, and boutique confectionery. The emotional tone evokes feelings of nostalgia, comfort, care, and sensorial delight—mirroring the aroma of baked goods, warm chocolate, and rich vanilla. 

The aesthetic blends **Tactile Softness** with **Refined Editorial Elegance**:
- Soft, generous surfaces that recall velvety frostings and delicate glazes.
- Warm, luminous backgrounds avoiding stark whites or sterile grays.
- Thoughtful, generous negative space to present gourmet creations with gallery-like culinary reverence.
- Micro-interactions that emphasize gentle ease and tactile delight rather than mechanical snaps.

## Colors
The palette takes direct inspiration from rich patisserie ingredients:
- **Primary (`#4A281E` - Hot Chocolate / Dark Truffle)**: A deep, velvety roasted cocoa brown used for primary actions, authoritative typography, and grounding elements. Provides exceptional contrast without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#F4C2C2` - Glaze Pink / Pastel Rose)**: A soft, romantic dusty rose for accents, badges, highlights, and subtle interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#C88A58` - Salted Caramel)**: A golden, warm confectionary tone used for secondary buttons, ratings, seasonal alerts, and artisanal tags.
- **Neutral (`#FAF6F0` - Vanilla Cream / Crème Pâtissière)**: The foundational backdrop, providing an ultra-warm canvas that makes imagery and rich brown typography stand out softly.

Supplementary surface tones derive from vanilla and butter tints (`#F2EBE1` for card surfaces, `#E8DECE` for delicate borders and dividers).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances high-end culinary editorial appeal with modern e-commerce utility:
- **Playfair Display**: Chosen for all display headers, section titles, and featured dessert names. Its refined serifs, graceful curves, and delicate thins echo artisanal calligraphy and luxury bakery menu design.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: Selected for body copy, navigational anchors, price tags, and forms. Its friendly humanist geometry retains warmth without distracting from editorial headings, remaining legible at small sizes across checkout flows and ingredient lists.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, converting to a single-column or 2-column card stream on mobile devices:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `3rem` outer margins. Content reaches a maximum reading width of 1280px for balanced breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` canvas margins.

Rhythm is deliberate: generous vertical padding (`space-xl`) isolates featured delicacies, recreating the uncluttered staging of boutique display counters.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical, pillowy confections and gentle natural lighting:
- **Warm Ambient Shadows**: Shadows do not use harsh achromatic black. Instead, they are tinted with diluted warm chocolate (`rgba(74, 40, 30, 0.07)` and `rgba(74, 40, 30, 0.12)`).
- **Soft Diffusion**: Shadow blurs are extra-diffused with minimal vertical displacement (e.g., `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(74, 40, 30, 0.08)` for cards and floating elements).
- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is reinforced by resting `#FFFFFF` or pale cream `#FFFDFB` containers directly on top of the `#FAF6F0` vanilla base, bordered by faint caramel-tinted strokes (`#E8DECE`).
- **Interactive Rise**: Upon hover, product cards lift gracefully by 3px while their warm chocolate shadow extends to `0 16px 32px -6px rgba(74, 40, 30, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
Roundedness level `2` provides welcoming, soft curves that evoke confections, macarons, and artisanal cake tiers:
- **Base Components**: Inputs, buttons, and badges leverage `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) border radii.
- **Cards & Modal Trays**: Employ `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px) corners, removing sharp, aggressive industrial edges.
- **Pill Accents**: Dietary tags (e.g., "Sem Glúten", "Artesanal") and icon buttons may adopt full pill radiuses (`9999px`) to emphasize a smooth, candy-like tactile feel.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid roasted chocolate (`#4A281E`) background, vanilla cream (`#FAF6F0`) text, `0.75rem` rounded corners. Hover transitions gently to caramel (`#C88A58`) with a diffused chocolate shadow.
- **Secondary**: Pastel rose (`#F4C2C2`) background, deep chocolate (`#4A281E`) text. Hover introduces a warm subtle glow.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent fill with a warm biscuit border (`#E8DECE`) and `#4A281E` label.

### Product Cards
- Contained in crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1rem` radius and a delicate `1px` border in `#E8DECE`.
- Imagery is framed with generous internal padding, presented on a soft pastel rose or muted cream background mask.
- Includes a dedicated area for price displayed in bold chocolate brown, complemented by an elegant seriffed dessert name.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Vanilla tint background (`#FFFDFB`), border in `#E8DECE`, rounded by `0.5rem`.
- Focus state triggers a subtle caramel outline (`#C88A58`) accompanied by a diffuse outer ring tint (`rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Smooth circular and softened square bounds with deep chocolate active fills and warm cream check indicators.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags with light pastel rose backgrounds (`#FCE8E8`) or warm honey tints (`#F8EFE3`), paired with chocolate labels to highlight ingredients (e.g., "Cacau 70%", "Feito à Mão").

### Special Components
- **Flavor Profile Gauge**: Subtle segmented bar using caramel and pastel rose fills to visualize sweetness, acidity, and intensity.
- **Gift Box Selector**: Interactive container cards displaying artisanal ribbon borders, custom card messaging options, and packaging previews.
